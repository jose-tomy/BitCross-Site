Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Rivo (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the App.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Your alarms, schedules, and app preferences are stored strictly locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal alarm logic data on external servers.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve app stability and performance, we use third-party analytics and crash reporting services. These services automatically collect anonymous usage data and crash logs. This data helps us identify bugs and improve the user experience, but it does not contain personally identifiable information (PII).

4. User Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback through the app, we collect the text you enter, along with basic diagnostic device information (such as your app version, operating system version, device model, time zone, and localized device region). This helps us effectively troubleshoot issues. This data is securely transmitted and stored strictly for customer support and quality assurance.

5. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes standard frameworks to deliver background audio, notifications, and local data persistence. We ensure that any third-party telemetry tools we integrate comply with strict privacy standards and exclusively handle anonymized data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@bitcross.app
